Welcome to the Arkansas Railroad Museum

Where Steel Rails Meet Southern Heritage

Step into the heart of American railroading at the Arkansas Railroad Museum, located in historic Pine Bluff. Housed in the former Cotton Belt (St. Louis Southwestern Railway) shops, our museum is a living tribute to the golden age of rail and the people who kept it running.

Explore Our Collection

  • Historic Locomotives: Marvel at our collection of meticulously restored steam and diesel locomotives, each with its own unique story.
  • Vintage Railcars: Step inside beautifully preserved passenger and freight cars that showcase the evolution of rail travel.
  • Interactive Exhibits: Engage with hands-on displays that bring the history of railroading to life for visitors of all ages.
  • Guided Tours: Join our knowledgeable guides for an in-depth look at our exhibits and the history of railroading in Arkansas.
  • Special Events: Participate in themed events, train rides, and educational programs throughout the year.

A Working Museum

The Arkansas Railroad Museum is not just a static display of history; it's a working museum where visitors can experience the sights, sounds, and smells of a bygone era. Our dedicated team of volunteers and staff work tirelessly to restore and maintain our collection, ensuring that the legacy of railroading continues to inspire future generations.

Great for Families & Groups

Whether you're a lifelong railfan or a curious kid seeing a steam engine for the first time, the Arkansas Railroad Museum offers something unforgettable for all ages. Group tours and school field trips are welcome!

Plan Your Visit

Whether you're a rail enthusiast, history buff, or simply looking for a unique family outing, the Arkansas Railroad Museum offers something for everyone. We look forward to welcoming you to the Arkansas Railroad Museum, where the spirit of the rails lives on!
